# The Imprint House: Competitor, Website & Creative Strategy

**Prepared:** 4 November 2026 · **Updated:** 4 November 2026 (website fixes applied, competitor links added)
**For:** Ruqaiyah, Marketing Head, Havels Group
**Website:** theimprinthouse.in

---

## 1. Summary

The Imprint House sells laser-engraved corporate gifts (keychains, pens, flasks, mugs, bottles and ready gift kits). The minimum order is 50 pieces, the mockup is free, prices include GST, and everything is engraved in-house.

**Key findings:**

1. **Print Deer is the main direct competitor.** Its ₹99 pen + keychain ad price is the 25-piece tier (₹2,475 ÷ 25). That's a bait price. Its "premium" 3-in-1 engraved set costs ₹264 per set at 50 pieces, almost the same as the Imprint House Essential kit (₹249).
2. **The biggest competitive gaps are minimum order and payment terms, not price.** Competitors accept orders from 2–10 pieces, and Print Deer offers COD with a ₹199 token. The Imprint House requires 50 pieces and 100% advance from first-time buyers.
3. **The website is solid but has trust gaps.** Policies are default templates that contradict the FAQ, the Legal Notice page is blank, support uses a personal Gmail, and there's no social proof.
4. **Winning creative angle:** permanence, precision, craft ("cut into metal"), shown through real process footage. Don't compete on discount tactics.
5. **Ads should send people to WhatsApp** (a click-to-WhatsApp campaign), not to a website "Purchase."

## 2. How the research was done (and its limits)

- **Meta Ad Library** was searched for every competitor named. For normal commercial ads in India, Ad Library does **not** show impressions, spend or conversions; those are only shown for political ads and EU ads. Nobody outside a brand can see its conversions.
- **The research tools can't watch ad videos or see ad images.** They return only headlines, dates and links.
- **Proxies used instead:**
  - **How long an ad has run.**
  - **Whether a brand keeps relaunching the same ad.** Brands don't keep paying for losers.
- **Competitor websites** were read in full for actual prices, offers and policies.
- **IndiaMart and B2B listings** were used for general market prices. These are often "starting from" prices that leave out GST, engraving setup, box and shipping.
- **Labels:** points marked *(judgement)* are strategist opinion, not measured data.

### How they sell
1. **Tiny minimums.** You can buy from 2–5 pieces.
2. **Low-risk payment.** COD with "Pay just ₹199 now," "Pay just 25% now," and 5% off for paying upfront.
3. **Pay first, design later.** Their team contacts you within 24–48 hours after you order. You can also order with text only if you don't have a logo.
4. **Fake discounts.** Crossed-out prices like ₹4,999 (88% off) and ₹4,975 (50% off).
5. **Fake urgency.** "331+ units sold in the last 1 day" appears on every product, even ones with zero reviews. "21–26 people viewing now."
6. **Big trust claims.** 10,000+ customers, 27,600+ orders, 620+ corporate clients, 4.9★. Logos of Inox, Radisson, Taj, Jai Vilas Palace and Red Tape.
7. **WhatsApp sales assistant** pop-up ("Ms. Yamini").
8. **Real product photos** (WhatsApp images of actual orders).
9. **Hindi ad headline:** "आपका Brand, हर Gift के साथ!" ("Your brand, with every gift"), relaunched several times.

### Their ads
- **The pen + keychain combo** ("Premium Personalized Gift Combo – Pen + Keychain" / "Personalized Keychain & Metal Pen Set") launched around 2 Oct, relaunched around 18 Oct and again around 1 Nov. **This is the strongest "this ad works" signal found.**
- The Hindi headline was relaunched around 1 and 3 Nov.
- "Premium Corporate Gifts with Custom Branding" ran from around 24 Oct (generic).

### Their weaknesses (your openings)
- **Delivery promises contradict each other:** "Dispatch in 24–48 hrs" on product pages, "7–10 working days" in the shipping policy, "within 7 days" on the homepage.
- **Return rules contradict each other:** the shipping policy allows 3-day returns if unopened; the refund policy says no returns on personalised products. The shipping policy links to another company's site, suggesting it was copied from a template.
- **Pricing errors:** a collection card shows ₹349 for 25 pieces vs ₹2,475 on the product page; a 25-piece bottle set costs more per piece than 12 pieces; a 500-keychain tier is listed at ₹1,89,500; a pen's "sale" price is higher than its "regular" price.
- **Trust claims don't hold up:** a 4.9★ claim but corporate products show "No reviews," and the testimonials are about polaroids and NFC cards.
- **Sloppy details:** a leftover "Are you 18+?" pop-up, typos, two phone numbers, a Gmail address.
- **No specialisation.** Corporate gifts sit next to T-shirts and salon standees.

### What they do better than The Imprint House (honestly)
1. Small orders are welcome.
2. Low risk to buy (COD / ₹199 token).
3. Instant checkout with visible quantity pricing.
4. Proof of scale (client logos, order counts, named team).
5. Real order photos.

### Pricing considerations (decision pending)
1. **Don't match ₹99.** It's close to wholesale cost and would force discount-store tactics.
2. **The ₹249 kit needs to show its value visibly:** both pieces engraved, boxed, GST included, free mockup, delivery included.

4. **Consider a lower first-order advance** (for example 30–50% instead of 100%).
5. **Consider an "entry" option:** an engraved pen + keychain without the box, at a lower price, with the boxed kit as the step up.

## 8. What creatives are working vs not

### Working
1. **Combos over single products.** Print Deer's pen + keychain combo was relaunched 3 times in a month.
2. **A specific price in the hook** (₹99, ₹449). It answers the buyer's first question.
3. **Hindi/Hinglish copy.** Print Deer keeps relaunching its Hindi headline.
4. **Sending people to WhatsApp.** Most of the category ends in "Chat with us."
5. **Real photos over studio perfection** *(judgement)*.

### Not working / avoid
- Diwali creatives after 8 November.
- Fake discounts and fake urgency counters.
- AI-generated product images *(judgement: they erode trust)*.
- Catalogue dumps (10 products in one ad).
- The generic line "corporate gifts with your logo."
- Hiding the price completely *(judgement: attracts people only fishing for prices)*.
- Consumer angles ("never lose your keys") that don't fit a 50-piece minimum.

## 9. Creative direction and visual identity

**Core idea:** *"Gifts with your name cut into metal."* Permanence, precision, craft.

- **Look:** dark, rich backgrounds (black, charcoal, deep wood), bright silver engraving catching the light, warm workshop lighting, extreme close-ups, real hands, real workshop, real client logos (with permission).
- **Feel:** crafted, solid and premium. Not flashy, not discount-y.
- **Sound signature:** the laser's hum and click, the box lid opening, a pen clicking.
- **On-screen text:** bold and short (6–7 words max). Always include "₹___ per person, GST included" (once pricing is final) and "Free mockup in 24 hrs · WhatsApp us."
- **Formats:** 9:16 video for Reels/Stories (main), 4:5 for feed images and carousels. Every video must work with the sound off, so add captions.

## 11. Campaign setup and testing plan

### Campaign type
- A **click-to-WhatsApp** campaign (Engagement or Leads objective, with WhatsApp as the destination).
- **Not the Sales objective yet.** With a 50-piece minimum, there won't be enough website purchases for Meta to learn from; it generally needs around 50 conversion events a week.

### What to measure
- **Cost per WhatsApp conversation.**
- **How many of those chats send their logo.** This is the quality signal: a logo means a real buyer.
- **How many become quotes, and then orders.**
- **Tracking:** set up website WhatsApp button clicks as a "Contact" event in Meta.

### First launch: 5 creatives
- Problem: "Drawer" (B1)
- Quality proof: "Faded vs forever" (B3)
- Emotion: key handover (B4)
- Trust: mockup vs reality (B8) or the core laser reveal (A1) until client orders exist
- Price: "50 kits, one invoice" (C14), once pricing is final

**Keep in reserve:** onboarding (B5) for January, vox pop (B12), and the workshop story (A6) once footage is ready.

### Testing rules
- Give every ad roughly equal spend at first.
- Don't judge an ad until it has spent at least 2–3 times your target cost for one good chat.
- Pause the bottom performers.
- Make 2–3 versions of each winner, changing only the first 2 seconds or the language (English vs Hinglish). This avoids ad fatigue (the same audience getting bored of the same ad) without reshooting.
