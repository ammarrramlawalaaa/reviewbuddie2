1What is the Amazon review policy for sellers in India?

The Amazon review policy for sellers comes down to one line: you can ask for a review, but you can never influence what the customer writes. No rewards, no asking only happy buyers, and no reviews from anyone connected to you.

That gap is exactly when most sellers break a rule without realising it.

Here's exactly where Amazon draws the line. Check your own setup against both lists.

✓Allowed

  • Request a Review button in Seller Central
  • Amazon Vine, if your product is eligible
  • A neutral insert asking for an honest review
  • Setup guides and warranty registration for every buyer

✕Not allowed

  • Free product, refund, cashback or gift card for a review
  • Asking only happy buyers, or sending unhappy ones to WhatsApp first
  • Asking for a positive review or a top rating
  • Reviews by you, your family, staff or a review group

Source: Amazon Seller Central, Customer Product Reviews policies

  1. Remove all of the product's reviews
  2. Block new reviews and ratings
  3. Delist the product permanently
  4. Suspend your seller account

2What do Indian sellers think is allowed but actually breaks the policy?

Most violations in India aren't big fraud. They're "everyone does it" habits that feel harmless but sit on Amazon's banned list. These five come up most often.

  1. 💬

    WhatsApp and Telegram review groups

    "Buy, review, get refunded later" is a paid review, even if someone else sends the refund.

  2. 💸

    "Review karo, cashback milega"

    Any benefit tied to a review: cashback, next-order discount, free gift, "₹100 Paytm for feedback".

  3. 👨‍👩‍👧

    Friends, family and staff

    Banned by name in Amazon's policy, even after a genuine purchase.

  4. 🚦

    The "contact us first" card

    "Happy? Review us. Problem? WhatsApp us." is review diversion.

  5. 🎨

    Variation stuffing

    Listing a new product as a "colour" of an old bestseller to borrow its stars.

If any of these is running in your business today, stop it before Amazon's systems find it. None of them needs bad intent to trigger a warning.

3What can you write on a package insert or thank-you card?

Amazon allows inserts that ask for an honest review in neutral words, the same way for every buyer. The moment a card asks for a good rating, offers something, or treats unhappy buyers differently, it crosses the line.

Asking for a review
✕"Loved it? Please leave us a great rating!"
✓"Your honest review helps other buyers decide."
Warranty registration
✕"Register your warranty and get ₹200 off. Just leave a review."
✓"Scan for the setup guide and warranty registration."
Customer support
✕"Not happy? Contact us before you review."
✓"Need help with setup? Here's how to reach us."

The pattern: safe lines help or inform, unsafe ones reward or filter. Here's a card you can print as it is.

5What should you do about an unfair 1-star review?

Report it only if it breaks Amazon's Community Guidelines, for example when it's about delivery or the seller rather than the product. An honest review, even a harsh one, will stay, and fighting it the wrong way creates a bigger problem.

✓Do

  • Tap "Report" with a short reason and the product link
  • Check if it's only about delivery: those get removed, FBA or not
  • Help the buyer via Buyer-Seller Messaging, as for any complaint

✕Don't

  • Ask the buyer to remove or change the review
  • Offer a refund or gift on the condition the review changes
  • Contact the buyer on WhatsApp or phone outside Amazon

Read your 1-stars for patterns. Three saying "couldn't figure out how to use it" is an instructions problem, not a review problem, so treat every honest 1-star as free product research.

6What changed in Amazon's review rules in 2026?

Two shifts matter for Indian sellers: Amazon split reviews across variations that work differently, and India tightened its e-commerce rules. Underneath both, fake reviews were already illegal under Indian consumer law.

  1. Feb–May 2026

    Variation review sharing

    Variations that work differently show only their own reviews. Build reviews per key variation.

  2. Notified 9 Sept 2026 · In force 1 Jan 2027

    E-Commerce (Amendment) Rules, 2026

    Stricter rules on dark patterns, discounts and seller disclosures, including ratings.

  3. BIS standard · Voluntary now

    IS 19000:2022 (online reviews)

    Bars editing reviews or discouraging negative ones. A mandatory version has been proposed.

  4. Already law

    Consumer Protection Act, 2019

    Fake reviews already count as an unfair trade practice.

Sources: Department of Consumer Affairs, Bureau of Indian Standards

Key takeaways

  • Ask for reviews, never shape them: no rewards, no "happy buyers only".
  • Family, staff and review groups can't review, even after genuine purchases.
  • Inserts are safe only with neutral wording every buyer gets.
  • Use Request a Review, Vine for launches, and better product help.
  • Report only guideline-breaking reviews; fix what honest 1-stars point to.
